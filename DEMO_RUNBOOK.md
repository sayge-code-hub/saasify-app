# Eka Staffing — Mahindra Finance demo runbook

Sign in to the isolated **Eka Staffing — Demo** workspace with `adityaranaworks@gmail.com`. Every seeded record is fictional and source data uses a `DEMO`/`demo_eka_` prefix.

## Suggested 10-minute story

1. **Settings → Company details**: introduce Eka Staffing as the supplier workspace, then open **Manage clients** to show Mahindra Finance and Ketan Jain.
2. **HRMS → All employees**: show five fictional technical consultants assigned to Mahindra Finance. Open Aarav Mehta to discuss compensation, client assignment, and employee records.
3. **Finances → Proposals**: open `EKA/PROP/2026/001`, an accepted five-person staffing proposal. Explain the proposal-to-PO handoff.
4. **Finances → Purchase orders**: show the five employee-linked POs. Highlight Meera Iyer's PO, expiring on 30 November 2026, as the renewal scenario.
5. **Finances → Invoices**: show three monthly invoices covering paid, part-paid, and sent collection states. Select an invoice to demonstrate employee-linked POs and invoice lines.
6. **Finances → Expense**: show paid, approved, and pending expense states. Edit an approved expense to demonstrate that it returns to pending review.
7. **Finances → Profitability**: lead with this screen. It combines invoiced revenue, consultant package cost, and approved expenses by client. Open Mahindra Finance and its projection view for the margin story.
8. **DMS**: show employee and client document groupings. The two seeded records are labelled demo placeholders; upload a real sample only if a live download is needed.
9. **Settings → Billing**: explain that the pilot is unrestricted today, POS is disabled, and subscription access can be enforced per customer later without an application release.
